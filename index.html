Financial intelligence infrastructure. One desktop terminal for live markets, research, portfolios, risk, derivatives, funds, macro and geopolitics — built on a single event-to-decision system.

How it works

From a headline to a decision, automatically.

Every terminal stops at what happened. Quantify keeps going.

  1. 01

    A filing lands

    It enters the Data Fabric with a canonical identifier, point-in-time timestamps and machine-readable data rights.

  2. 02

    The model updates

    Figures become facts carrying the sentence they were read from. The Model Compiler proposes the exact cells that change.

  3. 03

    The thesis re-scores

    Every assumption the change touches is re-scored — with its inputs, uncertainty and evidence in plain view.

  4. 04

    Exposure is recomputed

    The Portfolio Digital Twin traces first-, second- and third-order exposure through suppliers, customers and geographies.

  5. 05

    The decision is recorded

    Tested against the mandate, approved by a person, written to the Decision Ledger — and replayable forever.

What I believe

The principles the company is built on.

  1. 01

    Information is a commodity. Conviction isn't.

    Every terminal tells you what happened. The edge is knowing what it changes — in your model, your thesis, your book.

  2. 02

    Speed of reasoning beats number of tabs.

    The win isn't more screens. It's memory, reproducibility, and knowing your portfolio impact in minutes instead of afternoons.

  3. 03

    Evidence, or nothing.

    Every claim cites the line it came from. If it can't show its source, it doesn't ship.

  4. 04

    Institutional tools shouldn't need an institution.

    Fast, transparent, rigorous infrastructure — without economics that lock it behind the biggest firms.

  5. 05

    Foundations first. Features later.

    Every stage has an exit criterion. Nothing gets built on a layer that hasn't passed its own.

  6. 06

    Build it for the long run.

    Not a feature. Not a wrapper. Infrastructure that should still be running decades from now.
