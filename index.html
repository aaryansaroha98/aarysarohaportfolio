The founder

  


  

    

    

      
Electrical engineer by training. Quant by obsession. Builder by default.

      
I'm Aaryan — Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Quantify Terminal, and an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at IIT Jammu. My work sits where quantitative finance meets financial engineering: pricing and valuation frameworks, factor and risk models, market microstructure, and the data infrastructure required to run them at production scale.

      
I lead architecture and product directly — from ingestion to the analytics on the desk. A canonical data layer with machine-readable rights. A deterministic finance engine. Event-driven model and thesis updates. A native terminal that stays fast under institutional load.

      
Before Quantify, I shipped full-stack products since 2022: a live e-commerce store, the ticketing platform for IIT Jammu's film society, and a pile of experiments nobody asked for.
